How we can help

Whether you drive for daily life or run a fleet, motor insurance helps protect you against third-party claims and damage to your own vehicle.

We help you review policy options, excess levels and add-ons, so the cover reflects how you actually use your vehicle.

Typical covers

  • Third-party liability
  • Comprehensive own-damage cover
  • Theft and fire
  • Optional roadside and personal accident benefits

For general information only. Actual benefits, limits and exclusions vary by plan and are set out in the policy wording.
